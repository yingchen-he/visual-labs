Test Instructions
  1. Cover your LEFT eye and fixate on the red cross (+). Your line of sight should be perpendicular to the screen.
  2. Adjust the Starting Center Position until the dot fully disappears.
  3. Select a direction and click Move Forward until the dot re-appears. Record the boundary.
  4. Go back to center and record points for all 8 directions to unlock polygon boundary drawing.
  5. Once finished, switch to cover your RIGHT eye and change the settings accordingly.
