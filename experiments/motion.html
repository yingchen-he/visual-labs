Main Menu Motion Perception: Wagon Wheel Effect
Correspondence Problem & Wagon Wheel Instructions
  1. Click Move (Start) to begin rotating the wheel. Positive speeds rotate the wheel physically clockwise.
  2. The Speed of Wheel is represented as (deg/frame). Each frame lasts 100ms (i.e., 10 frames per second).
  3. Gradually increase the rotation speed. Notice how the perceived rotation increases, becomes ambiguous, and reverses direction.
  4. Toggle Show Dot to place a unique red marker on one spoke to resolve the spatial correspondence ambiguity and reveal the true motion.
